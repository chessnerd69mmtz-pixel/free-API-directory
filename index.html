What's new

The latest expansion adds 1,716 community-sourced providers from the maintained public-apis dataset, plus the earlier curated expansion. These records are explicitly labeled community-listed and are not presented as independently verified provider quotas. The expanded catalog covers AI inference and speech, AI observability, GPU compute, databases, vector search, scraping and browser automation, geospatial services, financial data, scientific research, bioinformatics, government data, media and more. New expansion records are explicitly marked as candidate / unverified until their current free-tier terms are checked against official provider sources.
